Koi Report Analyser V1.0

Load the KoiDiagnostics_<Computer>_<Timestamp>.txt report produced by KoiDiagnostics_Simple.ps1. The analyser shows what's working, what isn't, and what to do next.

Drop the report file here or choose it from your computer. Text files from the script (UTF-8 or UTF-16) are supported.

The report is read and analysed inside this browser tab. It is not uploaded or stored anywhere.
